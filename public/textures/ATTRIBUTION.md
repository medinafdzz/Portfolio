# Planet maps

Earth day, Earth night and Moon maps: Solar System Scope / INOVE.
Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Downloaded 2026-11-05. The original 2K JPG maps are unmodified; SPACEFOLIO
applies lighting, rotation, emissive night illumination and a procedural
atmosphere at render time. The maps are textures on real 3D spheres.
No reference image is used as a scene background or bundled with the app.
